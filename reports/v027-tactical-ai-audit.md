# v0.2.7 tactical infantry AI audit — in progress

Base main `6d61f9c` contains merged v0.2.6 (`c48c268`); verified before creating `feat/v027-tactical-infantry-overhaul`. Nothing is merged automatically.

## Existing architecture inspected

The existing simulation already has shared sight snapshots and uncertain bearings, bounded last-contact recon, expanded-map exploration, conditional one-soldier flanking, directional cover with a two-mover budget, injuries/medic route rules, grenade evasion and LMG deployment. New work extends these systems. No external dependencies or AI framework are added. Legacy and v0.2.5 modes retain old behavior by default; the new tactical layer defaults on for expanded mode and can be explicitly enabled by focused fixtures.

## M1 — cover postures

Implemented authoritative `full`, `peek`, and `sustained` postures in `src/postures.js`. `world.sight` now applies low-cover body/eye occlusion only to real full-posture units; plain terrain points and snapshots retain terrain LOS. A protected ray requires >=0.58 material protection. Full shelter prohibits firing, while flanks/grenades remain dangerous and solid obstacles remain solid in all postures. Reload/heavy suppression can extend shelter; contact expiration, movement, incapacitation and newly reported exposed flanks release it. Nonrandom role/id/pressure-dependent exposure windows avoid shared clocks and do not consume combat RNG. Medical work and grenade evasion precede the holding decision.

Perception does not mark a concealed low-cover position empty simply because no soldier is currently visible; its observed coordinates still freeze and expire. Rendering consumes the posture, lowers the body, and suppresses residual muzzle flashes in full cover. Inspector shows the actual posture and decision reason.

Validation: six new posture tests pass; existing 19 cover/mount, 17 presentation and 15 search tests pass. Main baseline suite: 178 passing tests. Twelve deterministic cover fixtures (seeds 2001–2012, exact inputs in JSON) run against frozen main and the final M1 source; all finish, no invalid solid-obstacle shots or friendly grenade hits. Failures found and corrected before the final rerun: exposed flank not releasing full shelter; previous shot flash persisting while hidden. The no-hidden-tracking test initially moved the soldier around the barricade and correctly reacquired it; the fixture was corrected to remain concealed instead of weakening LOS.

Paired M1 results (`v027-m1-before.json`, `v027-m1-after.json`): P50 41.5→77.0s, maximum 97.5→102.2s, navigation increments 0.5833→1.0000/match. Full cover 117.8583 and peek exposure 98.1833 soldier-seconds/match. More cover use is not claimed as general AI improvement: passive and exposed time also increase. This is a small feature-validation cohort, not the final 1,000-run audit. Existing exclusive activity accounting is unchanged; intentional full shelter may still count as passive under its conservative contribution rule. The audit's `stuck` counter is a stationary-without-direct-sight heuristic, not proof of a deadlock.

## M2 — bounded last-known suppression

`src/suppressive-fire.js` selects only radio snapshots aged 0.2–5s (LMG) / 0.2–2.5s (rifle), confidence >0.45, uncleared, in range and with a real firing lane. No live enemy position is read to choose the destination. LMG/rifle budgets are ten/two shots per observation timestamp, with actual magazine/cooldown/aim delay and friendly-lane clearance. Search/recon/care/maneuver priorities and full cover disallow speculative fire. Actual projectile resolution alone can intersect a live body on that fixed ray; concealed bodies can receive attenuated near-miss pressure through a low firing lane, never through a solid wall. Damage requires a valid physical hit and probability; full-cover concealment prevents direct damage along that protected lane. The inspector records the real task/shot budget and shot events drive the same muzzle/recoil system.

Four new tests plus all six posture and 19 existing cover/mount tests pass. Same twelve cover configurations compare M1 to M2: zero timeouts/invalid shots/friendly grenade hits; mean fourteen remembered-lane shots/match, navigation 1.0000→0.3333, P50 77.0→60.6s and maximum 102.2→98.9s. These small-cohort changes are not a statistical claim. All twelve independent repeat hashes match. The largest adverse case 2002 lengthens 77.9→88.0s: real peeking, suppression, reload and one cover-congestion call, not a wall-firing loop. Cases 2001/2004 also lengthen with real protected fire and pressure; their traces are retained. A unit fixture initially omitted the perception-populated visible-enemy arrays; the fixture was fixed, not the production perception contract. No speculative AI retuning was applied to the adverse seeds.

Raw M2 data: `v027-m2-after.json`; paired pre-M2 source/data: milestone M1 and `v027-m1-after.json`. M3–M7 and final integrated audits remain pending.


## M3 — support and maneuver execution

Added `src/fire-movement.js` around the existing conditional, geometry/risk-checked flank planner. An accepted plan assigns a rifle maneuver element and adaptive support roles favoring an available LMG; medics retain local care/security. Supporting shots must actually occur within the recent 1.5s window. A brief establishment wait cancels after 3s without pressure, rather than issuing an unsupported charge or leaving both elements waiting indefinitely. Completed/expired/dead-mover plans release roles, and maneuver duties prevent a cyclic peek/hold posture from swallowing a valid movement assignment. Small squads can evaluate a one-supporter maneuver, without requiring it or bypassing the same route risk. Existing injury, suppression and command checks remain.

Four new deterministic tests verify real movement after supporting shots, bounded cancellation, role cleanup, medical priority and a genuinely reachable two-survivor maneuver. All prior new tests and 17 existing coordination tests pass. Twelve paired complete movement scenarios (2001–2012) and twelve independent replays finish without timeouts; all before/after core hashes are identical. **No conditional flank triggered naturally in this small cohort.** It is regression evidence, not a claim that those twelve battles demonstrate flanking. The focused tactical fixture does exercise support and movement; the large specialized cohort must measure natural activation. Explicit expired-plan role cleanup was added before final validation to avoid stale maneuver duties. M4–M7 remain pending.


## M4 — adaptive spacing and directional security

Added `src/security.js`: existing search escorts choose loose-column, staggered, dispersed or compact-regroup offsets from route heading, local clearance and squad separation, with mode hysteresis and the existing reachable/distinct destination solver. There is no rigid lock or requirement for scouts to wait for formation. Travel heading is recorded from actual displacement; observation/weapon direction can differ. Role-offset scans cover forward/side/rear sectors with real terrain LOS and an approximately 160° distant observation cone; close awareness remains LOS-limited. Engagement focus follows only recent confirmed snapshots. Perception, empty-location clearing, exploration memory and search-objective completion use the same observation rule. Existing 360° legacy compatibility is preserved.

Seven new security tests pass, along with 15 search, 17 coordination, prior posture and suppression tests. Fixed a real integration issue: a loose column's intended support distance could exceed the former four-tile support band, causing unnecessary retargeting. A reached search objective also now requires actual observation rather than bare terrain LOS when outside close awareness. A previous search regression assumed the refreshed point always required an initial positive-Y step; distinct formation positions can correctly require a lateral first step, so the assertion now checks actual displacement and reduced distance to the assigned destination. No movement assertion was removed. A stale active suppression-task label after casualty/direct-priority changes was also cleared, with a new regression. An initial test selected an obstacle instead of a free landmark; the fixture was corrected.

Twelve paired expanded cases 2001–2012 and independent replays complete with exact replay hashes and no timeouts/invalid shots. P50 96.3→84.3s, P95/max 134.5→151.0s, navigation 2.25→3.1667/match and maximum squad spread 18.1283→18.9374 tiles. These adverse tail/navigation results remain visible. In 2011, the late medic is genuinely pinned while rifle recon and an opposing LMG continue combat; in 2002, an injured defensive medic faces real LMG reacquisition. These are not proof of permanent formation locks. Final large-cohort investigation remains required. Raw `v027-m4-initial.json` preserves the intermediate pre-objective-observation correction; `v027-m4-after.json` is the validated final M4 source. M5–M7 remain pending.


## M5 — bounded dangerous-road crossing

Added `src/crossings.js`, inspecting only the next twelve cells of an already planned route. A short across-road segment is gated only with known/uncertain threats, an exposed crossing lane and a currently protected waiting position. At most two soldiers (one in a small surviving squad) reserve a local crossing; the rest may observe/cover without losing their route. No crossing ritual runs on an empty map, along-road travel or unsafe exposed entry. Grenade evasion, wounds/heavy suppression and danger at the entry bypass the reservation. Dead/stopped/unentered reservations expire, completed crossings free slots, and the local gate has an eight-second lifetime. The route watchdog pauses for an explicit crossing wait; actual displacement/progress counters are not fabricated. Existing medical, cover, recon and collision solvers remain responsible for route choice. This is a basic gate on the existing risk-weighted route, not a new global cover-to-cover optimizer.

Five new tests pass plus seven security and five inherited urban-navigation tests. Corrected a completion-accounting defect before final simulations: retreat behind the entry must not count as a completed crossing; completion requires passing the actual exit. Inspector shows the time of the last crossing decision rather than presenting old history as a current hold. Twelve paired movement cases and independent replays finish with identical before/after core hashes: P50 83.7s, max 157.3s, navigation 0.75/match, zero invalid shots/timeouts. **No qualifying protected-entry gate completed naturally in these twelve cases.** Manual geometry/traffic fixtures verify actual admission, waiting, release and emergency behavior. The integrated audit must report activation rather than assuming it. M6–M7 remain pending.
